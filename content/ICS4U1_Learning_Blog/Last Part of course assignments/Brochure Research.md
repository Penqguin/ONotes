# University Research: University of Waterloo

## I. Admission to the Institution

### General Admission Requirements
*   **Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD):** Completion of a minimum of six Grade 12 U or M courses.
*   **Core Prerequisite Courses:**
    *   English (ENG4U)
    *   Advanced Functions (MHF4U)
    *   Calculus and Vectors (MCV4U)
*   **Proof of English Language Proficiency:** Required for students whose first language is not English and who have not studied in an English-language school system for the last four years.

### Specific Admission Requirements for Computer-Related Programs
*   **Computer Science (CS):** 
    *   Requirements: Core prerequisites + one other Grade 12 U/M course.
    *   Competitive average: Mid-to-high 90s.
    *   *Note:* Participation in the Euclid Mathematics Contest and the Canadian Computing Competition (CCC) is highly recommended.
*   **Computer Engineering (CE):**
    *   Requirements: Core prerequisites + Chemistry (SCH4U) + Physics (SPH4U).
    *   Competitive average: Low 90s.
    *   *Note:* An online video interview is strongly recommended as part of the Admission Information Form (AIF).
*   **Software Engineering (SE):**
    *   Requirements: Core prerequisites + Chemistry (SCH4U) + Physics (SPH4U).
    *   Competitive average: Mid-90s.
    *   *Note:* Online video interview and proof of programming experience are **mandatory**.
*   **Computing and Financial Management (CFM):**
    *   Requirements: Core prerequisites + two other Grade 12 U/M courses.
    *   Competitive average: Low-to-mid 90s.

### Grade Stipulations
*   **High School:** Most applicants require a minimum individual grade of 70% in required courses, but the competitive entry average for these programs is significantly higher (90%+).
*   **To Stay in the Program (Maintenance):**
    *   **CS:** Minimum 60% major average and 60% cumulative average. 70% cumulative often required to stay in the Co-op stream.
    *   **Engineering (CE/SE):** Minimum 60% term average to progress. Falling below 60% or failing more than two courses may require repeating a term.
    *   **CFM:** Minimum 70% major average (CS/AFM courses) and 65% cumulative average.

### Location, Campus Information, & Attractive Features
*   **Location:** Waterloo, Ontario, Canada (the "Silicon Valley North").
*   **Campus Hubs:**
    *   **Davis Centre (DC):** The main hub for CS students, featuring the Math & Computer library and "Bit-Bucket" cafe.
    *   **Engineering 5 & 7 (E5/E7):** Home to the Student Design Centre, Robohub (autonomous robot testing), and the "Bridge" study space.
    *   **The Tatham Centre (TC):** Dedicated building for the largest co-op program in North America, with over 130 interview rooms.
*   **Attractive Features:**
    *   **Co-op Program:** Earn up to 2 years of professional experience at top companies like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon.
    *   **Velocity:** A world-class startup incubator that helps students launch their own businesses.
    *   **Community:** Active clubs like the Computer Science Club (CSC) and Engineering Society (EngSoc) host hackathons and technical workshops.

### Length of Program
*   **Duration:** 4.8 to 5 years (including 5–6 paid co-op work terms alternating with academic terms).

### Cost per Year (Estimates)
*   **Domestic Students:** ~$15,000 – $17,000 CAD (Tuition for CS/Engineering) + ~$15,000 living expenses.
*   **International Students:** ~$60,000 – $73,000 CAD (Tuition for CS/Engineering) + ~$20,000 living expenses.

## II. 4 Computer-Related Areas of Study Offered

### 1. Computer Science (CS)
*   **Description:** Focuses on the theoretical foundations of information and computation, along with practical techniques for their implementation and application. Topics include algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence, and software systems.
*   **Electives/Options:** Specializations in AI, Bioinformatics, Data Science, or Human-Computer Interaction. Includes the world-renowned Co-op option.

### 2. Computer Engineering (CE)
*   **Description:** Bridges the gap between hardware and software. Students learn about circuits, microprocessors, and computer architecture, as well as software development, networking, and security.
*   **Electives/Options:** Technical electives in robotics, embedded systems, and wireless communications. Mandatory Co-op.

### 3. Software Engineering (SE)
*   **Description:** A joint program between the Faculty of Mathematics and the Faculty of Engineering. It focuses on the entire software development lifecycle, emphasizing engineering principles for building large-scale, reliable, and secure software systems.
*   **Electives/Options:** Cohort-based learning where students stay with the same group. Options for electives in cloud computing, cybersecurity, and real-time systems. Mandatory Co-op.

### 4. Computing and Financial Management (CFM)
*   **Description:** A unique interdisciplinary program combining Computer Science with Accounting and Financial Management. It prepares students for the intersection of finance and technology (Fintech).
*   **Electives/Options:** Students take professional-grade finance courses and utilize Bloomberg Terminals. Electives in financial modeling, quantitative finance, and software engineering. Mandatory Co-op.

## III. The Co-operative Education Program (Co-op)

### Overview
Waterloo is home to the world's largest co-op program, allowing students to alternate between four-month academic terms and four-month paid work terms. Most computer-related programs at Waterloo are either mandatory co-op or offer a strong co-op stream.

### Key Features
*   **Work Experience:** Students typically complete 5 to 6 work terms, gaining up to 2 years of professional experience before graduation.
*   **Earnings:** Co-op students at Waterloo earn a competitive salary, which helps significantly with tuition and living expenses. Average earnings for computer science and engineering students are among the highest.
*   **The Tatham Centre (TC):** A dedicated building on campus for co-op, hosting thousands of interviews every term with employers from around the globe.
*   **PD Courses:** Students take Professional Development (PD) courses to improve their resume writing, interviewing, and workplace communication skills.

### Benefits of Waterloo Co-op
*   **Resume Building:** Graduate with a resume that already includes major tech companies and startups.
*   **Networking:** Build a professional network and often secure full-time job offers before graduation.
*   **Career Exploration:** Try out different roles (e.g., Software Developer, Data Scientist, Product Manager) in various industries.
*   **Financial Independence:** Use earnings to pay for education; many students are able to graduate with little to no debt.

### Top Employers
Waterloo students are highly sought after by top-tier tech companies, including:
*   **Big Tech:** Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Meta.
*   **Finance & Fintech:** Bloomberg, Jane Street, Manulife, major banks (RBC, TD).
*   **Startups & Local Gems:** Shopify, OpenText, and numerous startups in the Waterloo-Toronto tech corridor.
